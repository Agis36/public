
# Privacy Policy for MathQuest AI

**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction
**MathQuest AI** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of children and parents. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data in our mobile application designed for children and adults.

**Summary:**
*   No account registration, email address or phone number is required.
*   We use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage and improve the learning experience.
*   We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and Premium access.
*   We do **NOT** show advertisements.
*   All game progress, profiles and learning reports are stored **locally on your device**.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Information We Process
We do not require names, email addresses or phone numbers to use MathQuest AI. Profile nicknames are stored locally and are not included in our custom analytics events.

Our service providers process certain information described below, including app-instance identifiers, app interactions, approximate location and purchase information. These identifiers can distinguish an app installation even when we do not know the user's real name.

### B. Local Data Storage
The following data is stored locally on your device using `localStorage`:
*   User profile names (nicknames) and avatars.
*   Game settings (sound, language, voice speed).
*   Game progress (scores, stars, streaks).
*   Per-profile learning history, including session results, themes, difficulty, hint use and progress reports.
*   Parental PIN code.

If you delete the app or clear the app data, this information is permanently lost because we do not sync it to a cloud server.

### C. Microphone Usage & Voice Data
MathQuest AI allows users to answer questions using their voice.
*   **Usage:** The microphone is accessed only when the "Microphone" button is active during gameplay.
*   **Processing:** Voice data is processed in real-time by your device's operating system (Android Speech Services or Apple Speech Recognition).
*   **Storage:** We do **not** record, save, or upload audio recordings to our servers.

### D. AI Question Generation (Google Gemini API)
MathQuest AI uses Google's Gemini AI to generate unique math questions.
*   **What is sent:** When a game starts, we send an anonymous prompt (e.g., "Generate 5 math questions about Space for Age 7") to the API.
*   **What is NOT sent:** We do not send child names, voice data, location, or any personal identifiers to the AI service.

### E. Usage Analytics — Firebase Analytics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how MathQuest AI is used and improve its features.

Information processed includes:
*   An automatically generated app-instance identifier.
*   App and device information, such as app version and operating system.
*   Approximate location derived from IP addresses, rather than GPS.
*   Screen views and interactions, including practice-session starts and completions, question and correct-answer counts, Premium-screen views, and purchase or restore outcomes.
*   Purchase and subscription events where available through the SDK or configured integrations.

Our custom analytics events do not contain child profile names, profile identifiers, question text, photographs or voice recordings.

Our analytics configuration disables advertising personalization and denies advertising-related consent signals. Analytics records are separate from the detailed progress reports stored locally on the device.

### F. Purchases and Subscriptions — RevenueCat
We use RevenueCat and the relevant app store to process and manage purchases, validate transactions, restore purchases and determine Premium access.

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information, transaction identifiers and an automatically generated anonymous app-user identifier. We also provide the Firebase app-instance identifier to support purchase analytics.

Payment-card details are handled by the app store and are not available to us through RevenueCat.

## 3. User and Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)
Our app is designed to comply with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

*   We do not collect personal data from users.
*   We do not use behavioral advertising.
*   We do not allow users to share personal information publicly.
*   The "Settings" menu is protected by a Parental PIN gate to prevent children/users from modifying app configurations or deleting data accidentally.
*   Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions or requests. Please do not send a child's name, photographs, voice recordings or other personal information when contacting support unless necessary to address the request.

## 4. Third-Party Services
